The method

It makes the agent behave less like a slot machine and more like a junior designer with a process.

Huashu starts from context, freezes brand facts, chooses a visual school, builds actual HTML, then verifies and critiques before delivery.

AskLogo, screenshots, product shots, colors, fonts and brand rules before guessing.
FindOfficial sources first: brand pages, press kits, product pages and launch media.
FreezeWrite a brand spec so the work has stable inputs instead of vibe memory.
BuildHTML prototypes, decks, animations and infographics with real interaction where needed.
ReviewScore hierarchy, craft, function, coherence and originality before calling it done.
Positioning

For people who want design output without opening a design tool.

Good fit

  • Launch videos, product animations and explainers that need to ship quickly.
  • Clickable prototypes for app or web concepts when Figma fidelity is not required.
  • Board decks, data pages and infographics where exportable files matter.
  • Design direction exploration: three distinct routes instead of one generic answer.

Not pretending

  • It is not a replacement for a senior art director on major brand systems.
  • It does not promise layer-perfect Figma or Keynote round-tripping.
  • It will not invent brand assets when official material is required.
  • It is an 80-point skill: practical, fast, open, and honest about its limits.
